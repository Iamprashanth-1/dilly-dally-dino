# AMUHUDU — Series Bible & Storyboard

> An Instagram Reels animated series. 8 episodes × 30–45 seconds, vertical 9:16.
> Style: soft, semi-realistic stylized 3D (reference: Blender Studio's *Spring* — rounded shapes, warm natural light, painterly greens).

---

## 1. Logline

On a quiet hill-station village called **Amuhudu**, a lonely 9-year-old boy spends his days talking to the hills — until a family from the city arrives with a son his age, and one runaway ball turns two shy strangers into best friends.

**Theme:** loneliness → friendship. Every episode is a small step out of solitude.
**Tone:** gentle, warm, a little funny. No villains. The "antagonist" is silence/loneliness itself.
**Emotional arc (season):** Aru alone → curious → tentative friendship → bonded family → belonging.

---

## 2. Setting

- **Amuhudu** — a small village on a hill station: misty mornings, terraced slopes, clay-tile roofs, a banyan tree at the chowk, a winding stone path up to **Hilltop Point** (the recurring "special place").
- Recurring locations (to be modeled as environment assets):
  1. `LOC_Hilltop` — big view of valleys, one lonely bench/tree. Aru's spot.
  2. `LOC_Chowk` — village square with banyan tree, teashop, bus stop.
  3. `LOC_DevHouse` — the new family's house (fresh paint, city luggage).
  4. `LOC_Path` — the stone path between village and Dev's house.
- **Time of year:** post-monsoon — everything green, low golden light, occasional mist. Perfect for warm, cinematic reels.

## 3. Characters (already built in `characters.blend`)

| Character | Role | Personality | Design / Animation notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Aru** (9) | Protagonist, village boy | Quiet, imaginative, talks to hills & the dog; secretly hopes for a friend | Curly hair, red t-shirt, blue shorts. Moves slow, sits curled up. Blink/smile shape keys on face. Loneliness = small body language (shoulders in). |
| **Dev** (9) | The new boy | City kid: energetic, chatty, a bit shy at first, owns a red ball | Side-parted neat hair, yellow shirt, white shoes. Moves fast, big gestures. Contrast with Aru: motion vs stillness. |
| **Ammamma** | Aru's grandmother | Wise, warm, teasing; sees everything | White hair bun, teal saree, round glasses. Slow deliberate movements, hands behind back. Comic relief + emotional anchor. |
| **Latha** | Dev's mother | Friendly, settles in fast, befriends Ammamma | Maroon saree with gold shawl. |
| **Ravi** | Dev's father | Jovial, always carrying a box during Ep2 | Blue shirt, dark trousers. |
| **Bujji** | Village stray dog | The matchmaker; adopts Dev immediately | Golden fur, red collar. Fast wagging tail = happiness meter of every scene. |

**Shape keys available on every human face:** `Blink`, `Smile`, `Sad`, `Open` (mouth), `Brow.Raise` — enough for full expression acting on reels.

## 5. Recurring visual language

- **Loneliness** = wide static shots, Aru small in frame, cool morning light.
- **Friendship** = tighter shots, warm golden light, movement, Bujji in frame.
- The **red ball** appears in every episode somewhere (Easter egg for followers).
- Every episode opens with 2s of beauty (hook) and ends with a text card teasing the next.

## 6. Production pipeline

1. **Assets done:** ✅ 6 rigged characters (`characters.blend`).
2. **Next:** environment blockout (`LOC_Hilltop`, `LOC_Chowk`, `LOC_DevHouse`, `LOC_Path`) → props (bench, banyan, teashop, plates, stones, red ball, paper boats).
3. **Per episode:** link characters + environment from library files → block animation with the rigs → polish face shape keys → light (sun + mist volume) → render.
4. **Audio:** VO recorded first, animation timed to waveform. Caption text burned in edit, not in render.
